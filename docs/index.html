Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on roofing costs, lifespans, insurance claims, and our installation standards.

Roofing costs depend primarily on square footage, pitch/steepness, number of layers being removed, and the materials chosen. On average, standard architectural shingle replacements range between $7,500 and $18,000 for standard residential homes. Premium metal or slate systems range from $18,000 to $35,000+. We provide 100% free, fixed-price itemized estimates before any contract is signed.
Over 90% of standard single-family residential roof replacements are completed in just 1 to 2 days by our large certified crews. We schedule around weather windows, protect your landscape and gutters with specialized nets, and conduct full magnetic cleanups daily before leaving the job site.

We provide high-resolution drone photo evidence, meet the adjuster directly on your roof, and help ensure your claim covers all required code upgrades, gutters, and damaged decking.
